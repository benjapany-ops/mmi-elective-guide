STUDY GUIDE · INTERNATIONAL MEDICAL ELECTIVE

MMI Interview Preparation

คู่มือเตรียมสัมภาษณ์นักศึกษาแพทย์ · English questions & answers + Thai memory cues

12 Stations · Canada (Quebec) & Germany · 26 October 2026

Universal response structure — C.A.R.E.

C — Clarify: Understand the situation
A — Assess: Consider risks, stakeholders and constraints
R — Respond: Give concrete, responsible actions
E — Evaluate: Reflect and follow up
คำถามแรงจูงใจให้ใช้ Purpose → Evidence → Actions → Impact แทน

12 interview stations

STATION 01 · MOTIVATION
Why do you want to join an international medical elective?
WHY → LEARN → GROW
SAMPLE ANSWER
I want to experience a different healthcare setting, strengthen cultural humility and communication, and bring practical lessons into my future practice.
THAI MEMORY CUE
บอกเป้าหมายที่เฉพาะเจาะจงและเหตุผลที่การไปต่างประเทศช่วยเติมเต็ม
STATION 02 · IMPACT
How will this elective benefit you, your team, your university, and Thailand?
ME → TEAM → UNIVERSITY → COUNTRY
SAMPLE ANSWER
Personally, I hope to become more adaptable. I will share lessons with peers, represent my university professionally, and explore which ideas could be adapted responsibly to Thailand.
THAI MEMORY CUE
ตอบให้ครบสี่ระดับ พร้อมกิจกรรมหลังกลับ เช่น peer-sharing session
STATION 03 · CLINICAL ETHICS
What if a supervisor asks you to perform a procedure beyond your competence?
SAFETY → LIMITS → SUPERVISION
SAMPLE ANSWER
I would explain my current experience honestly, prioritize patient safety, and ask to observe or participate only within my competence, with appropriate supervision and patient consent.
THAI MEMORY CUE
อย่ารับทำหัตถการเกินความสามารถเพราะเกรงใจ
STATION 04 · CULTURAL SENSITIVITY
What if a practice abroad conflicts with your values?
OBSERVE → UNDERSTAND → ACT
SAMPLE ANSWER
I would avoid quick judgments, ask about the context, and raise concerns through appropriate channels if patient rights or safety were at risk.
THAI MEMORY CUE
เปิดใจต่อวัฒนธรรม แต่ไม่ละเลยหลักจริยธรรม
STATION 05 · COMMUNICATION
What if you misunderstand important clinical information?
ADMIT → CLARIFY → CONFIRM
SAMPLE ANSWER
I would acknowledge uncertainty, ask the team to clarify the details, and verify the information before presenting it.
THAI MEMORY CUE
ไม่เดาข้อมูลผู้ป่วย
STATION 06 · TEAM CONFLICT
What if a teammate says Thai students are too quiet to contribute?
PAUSE → LISTEN → DISCUSS
SAMPLE ANSWER
I would manage my initial reaction, ask what led to that impression, and explain different communication styles while remaining open to useful feedback.
THAI MEMORY CUE
รับฟังโดยไม่ปกป้องตัวเองทันที
STATION 07 · PRIORITIZATION
What if you face a housing problem before a major presentation and a peer needs urgent help?
SAFETY → URGENCY → RESPONSIBILITY
SAMPLE ANSWER
I would assess immediate safety risks, contact the relevant coordinator, secure safe accommodation, and communicate early about the presentation.
THAI MEMORY CUE
ประเมินความปลอดภัยก่อน แล้วจัดลำดับความเร่งด่วน
STATION 08 · RESILIENCE
What if you feel less knowledgeable than other international students?
ACCEPT → ADAPT → IMPROVE
SAMPLE ANSWER
I would acknowledge my feelings, set small learning goals, seek feedback, and focus on progress rather than comparison.
THAI MEMORY CUE
ยอมรับความรู้สึก ตั้งเป้าหมาย และขอคำปรึกษา
STATION 09 · INTEGRITY
What if a friend posts identifiable patient information online?
RECOGNIZE → ADDRESS → REPORT
SAMPLE ANSWER
I would ask for the post to be removed immediately and follow the hospital’s reporting procedure for a possible privacy breach.
THAI MEMORY CUE
ต้องจัดการการละเมิดความลับตามระเบียบ ไม่ใช่แค่ลบรูป
STATION 10 · SELECTION
Why should we select you?
STRENGTH → EVIDENCE → CONTRIBUTION
SAMPLE ANSWER
I would offer specific evidence of adaptability and responsibility, then explain how I will use the opportunity and share what I learn.
THAI MEMORY CUE
ยกหลักฐานจริง ไม่กล่าวว่าตนดีกว่าผู้อื่น
STATION 11 · RESEARCH INTEREST
What research interests you, and why is your host institution a good fit?
INTEREST → FIT → LEARNING → APPLICATION
SAMPLE ANSWER
I would identify a focused research question, check relevant faculty and projects, define feasible learning goals, and consider how the knowledge may be useful in Thailand.
THAI MEMORY CUE
เชื่อมความสนใจเข้ากับทีมวิจัยและโอกาสที่เข้าถึงได้จริง
STATION 12 · COUNTRY FIT
How would you choose between Quebec, Canada, and Germany?
RESEARCH → FIT → OPPORTUNITY → IMPACT
SAMPLE ANSWER
I would compare research groups, supervisors, current projects, language requirements, and what visiting students can actually do, rather than relying on reputation alone.
THAI MEMORY CUE
Quebec อาจต้องใช้ภาษาฝรั่งเศส เยอรมนีอาจต้องใช้เยอรมัน ตรวจเงื่อนไขโครงการจริง

Research & destination deep dive

I.F.L.A. — Interest → Fit → Learning → Application
Q–M–F–I — Research Question → Method → Feasibility → Impact

Quebec: Explore host-specific strengths in youth mental health, transcultural psychiatry or community care; check French-language and elective eligibility requirements.
Germany: Explore specific research groups in digital mental health, neuroscience or AI-assisted clinical research; check German-language and supervision requirements.
เลือกตามอาจารย์และโครงการที่รับจริง ไม่ใช่ภาพรวมชื่อเสียงประเทศ

Challenging follow-up questions

1. Why Quebec rather than another Canadian province?
2. Why Germany rather than Canada for this research question?
3. Which supervisor or current project would you approach, and why?
4. What would you do if no research position were available?
5. How will you measure the benefit to your university?
6. What can you contribute to the host team?
7. What if the clinical elective offers no formal research time?
8. What if you disagree with your overseas supervisor?

Two-minute practice rhythm

0–20s: Define the issue · 20–60s: Explain reasoning · 60–100s: Give specific actions · 100–120s: Reflect and conclude.

Preparation guide only. Verify each institution’s current elective, research and language requirements before applying.
